02 · TURNING POINT

Trust had to come before intelligence. Early UserTesting uncovered hesitation around placing sensitive investment data into a single digital environment.

RESEARCH INSIGHT · USERTESTING

Showing protection beats claiming security

While clients valued consolidated data, hesitation persisted until the interface visibly demonstrated how their sensitive investment information was governed and protected—rather than simply stating it was secure.

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Don't ask users to trust the AI before they trust the platform

Establishing foundational platform trust and data transparency first became the prerequisite for introducing any intelligent or AI-assisted capabilities.

03 · PRODUCT STRATEGY

AI that assists, rather than decides. Introduced as an intentional augmentation layer—never the center of the product.

1. AI Surfaces Information → 2. User Evaluates It → 3. Professional Applies Judgement
SME & MANAGER INTERVIEWS

Reducing effort without interfering with expertise

Through interviews with investment managers and subject-matter experts, we pinpointed high-friction synthesis tasks where AI could accelerate discovery while leaving interpretation untouched.

HIGH-CONSEQUENCE ENVIRONMENT

Clear boundary around professional agency

In institutional finance, decisions carry significant weight. Keeping AI clearly framed as supporting evidence ensured professionals remained in full control.

04 · FUND PROFILE ARCHITECTURE

Designing depth without overwhelming the user. Rebuilt one of the product's densest surfaces around hierarchy, scannability, and progressive disclosure.

PILLAR 01 · HIERARCHY

Overall picture first

Countered the instinct to expose every metric at once by establishing clear visual anchors so investors grasp fund posture immediately.

PILLAR 02 · SCANNABILITY

Visual cues over walls of text

Replaced dense blocks of financial prose with structured visual groupings and clear typographic contrast for rapid comparison.

PILLAR 03 · PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE

Depth on demand

Preserved full institutional rigor underneath clean summary layers so specialists can drill deeper whenever needed without upfront cognitive overload.

THE RESULT

From fragmented information to one trusted experience. Unified portfolio data, investment-manager details, documents, analytics, and AI assistance into one coherent client platform.

"In financial UX, trust isn't a feature. It's part of the experience."
