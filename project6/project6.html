QUICK SUMMARY

Clinical AI Documentation - TL;DR

  • The Challenge: Documentation competes with patient attention, yet clinicians cannot accept plausible-sounding AI text without knowing what it used or where uncertainty lies.
  • Bounded Scope: Limited AI strictly to Capture → Organise → Summarise → Retrieve-removing repetitive charting while keeping diagnosis, prescriptions, and final approval with the clinician.
  • Trust & Verification: Visually separated Patient Said, AI Generated, and Clinician Accepted states across a Generate → Review → Verify → Approve loop with accessible, non-color-only cues.

01 · THE CHALLENGES

Balancing attention and trust. The opportunity wasn't to automate clinical judgement-it was to remove repetitive documentation while keeping interpretation and approval with the clinician.

CHALLENGE 01 · ATTENTION

Documentation competes with patient care

Clinicians need to listen, ask questions, make decisions, and maintain human connection while simultaneously capturing accurate details for the clinical record.

CHALLENGE 02 · TRUST

AI introduces a verification problem

A healthcare professional cannot accept an AI output just because it sounds plausible-they must see what the AI generated, what source it used, and whether anything was misunderstood.

CHALLENGE 03 · BOUNDARIES

Automation has a clear boundary

Clinical judgement cannot be automated. AI belongs on repetitive charting tasks, leaving interpretation, diagnosis, and final approval strictly with the clinician.

02 · WORKFLOW AUDIT & SCOPE

Started with the clinical workflow rather than "What can AI do?"-mapping where effort goes during a consultation to separate charting from decision-making.

AI ASSISTANT · 4 BOUNDED CAPABILITIES

03 · INTERACTION DESIGN

Making AI-generated information distinguishable. Visually separating three distinct types of information prevents generated drafts from being mistaken for verified clinical facts.

STATE 01 · SOURCE

What the patient actually said

Direct consultation transcript quotes anchored to the encounter so clinicians can read raw patient statements verbatim.

STATE 02 · DRAFT

What the system generated or inferred

Clearly labeled AI-drafted summaries and extractions-visually distinct so unreviewed suggestions never blend into the medical record.

STATE 03 · VERIFIED

Reviewed and accepted by clinician

Explicitly confirmed clinical entries that only enter the final record after human review and sign-off.

04 · VERIFICATION & CONTROL

AI proposes. The clinician decides. Every important piece of information traces back to the consultation inside an explicit human-in-the-loop workflow.

1. Generate → 2. Review → 3. Verify → 4. Approve
CONTROL 01 · TRACEABILITY

Source-linked verification

Instead of presenting an AI note as a finished answer, each key point links back to the consultation so clinicians verify facts rather than relying on AI confidence alone.

CONTROL 02 · ITEM-LEVEL AGENCY

Accept, edit, or remove

Clinicians retain granular control over every AI-generated item-accepting accurate lines, editing clinical phrasing, or removing irrelevant suggestions.

CONTROL 03 · NO AUTO-SAVE

Deliberate sign-off

Replacing automatic saving with Generate → Review → Verify → Approve ensures nothing enters the record without clinical accountability.

05 · UNCERTAINTY & ACCESSIBILITY

Don't hide uncertainty behind a polished answer. Making gaps actionable and building accessibility into every state keeps high-stakes workflows safe.

CONSTRAINT 01 · TRANSPARENCY

Actionable uncertainty pathways

When information is missing, contradictory, or unclear, the interface surfaces it visibly and provides direct pathways to review manually or inspect the original transcript-never pretending the system is always right.

CONSTRAINT 02 · INCLUSIVE DESIGN

Accessibility at every step

Designed around clear visual hierarchy, full keyboard accessibility, readable transcription, and redundant visual cues so color is never the sole signal for AI states or warnings.

OUTCOME

A model for responsible AI in clinical workflows-helping clinicians spend less time documenting and more time reviewing, reasoning, and caring for the patient.

"AI doesn't need to replace the clinician to be useful. It just needs to make the clinician's work easier - without taking away their control."

EFFICIENCY Less Admin Effort

Automates repetitive charting so clinicians stay present with patients.

OVERSIGHT Human-in-the-Loop
