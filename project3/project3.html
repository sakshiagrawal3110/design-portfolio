OpsPilot — TL;DR

  • The Gap: Support teams don't lack metrics—they lose context switching between BI alerts, ticketing tools, spreadsheets, and Slack threads when SLA or CSAT dips.
  • The Loop: Anchored around Monitor → Investigate → Assign → Track → Resolve as an intelligence layer above existing helpdesks—with mandatory ownership or explicit dismissal.
  • Craft & KPIs: Exception-first desktop triage, inline root-cause side-drawers, and one-click escalation to tracked action items; measured via resolution-in-target rate, time to root cause, ownership coverage, and reduced tool switching (figures redacted under NDA).

02 · DEFINE

"How might we connect performance monitoring directly to root-cause investigation and tracked ownership?" Anchored around Monitor → Investigate → Assign → Track → Resolve.

1. Monitor → 2. Investigate → 3. Assign → 4. Track → 5. Resolve
BOUNDARY 01 · INTEGRATION

Not another ticketing system

Sits above existing helpdesks as an intelligence and coordination layer, ingesting signals without forcing teams to migrate their ticketing stack.

BOUNDARY 02 · FOCUS

Not a generic BI builder

Deprioritized infinite chart or custom SQL builders in favor of opinionated operational health views that tie anomalies directly to actionable queues.

BOUNDARY 03 · ACCOUNTABILITY

Ownership is mandatory

Anomalies cannot sit as passive red numbers. Every investigated issue becomes a tracked corrective action with a target window—or is explicitly dismissed with a reason.

03 · IDEATE & WORKFLOWS

Auditing 5 support and analytics platforms showed analytics tools are built for reading while trackers are built for doing—OpsPilot bridges both without context switching.

EXPLORATION 01 · INVESTIGATION

Inline root-cause drilldown

Instead of exporting CSVs to diagnose a metric dip, clicking an anomaly opens a contextual side-drawer pre-filtered to affected ticket clusters, regions, or agent queues.

Anomaly Alert → Filtered Side-Drawer (Clusters · Regions · Queues)
EXPLORATION 02 · HANDOFF

One-click escalation to Action Item

Once a root cause is spotted (such as a checkout issue spiking billing tickets), leads convert the investigation snapshot straight into a tracked action item with owner, priority, and resolution window.

Root-Cause Snapshot → Tracked Action Item (Owner · Priority · Window)

04 · PROTOTYPE & CRAFT

Designed for high-density desktop scanning, rapid incident triage, and audit-ready operational history.

01 · TRIAGE VIEW

Exception-first dashboard

Surfaces active operational anomalies ranked by customer impact and SLA risk instead of burying leads in vanity metrics.

02 · STATE VISIBILITY

Persistent resolution timeline

Tracks every issue across Investigating → Assigned → In Progress → Blocked → Resolved alongside elapsed time against its target window.

03 · SINGLE RECORD

Audit-ready context

Keeps metric spike, root-cause notes, and final resolution together—eliminating manual weekly "ops review" slide decks.

05 · TEST & VALIDATION

Structured a focused usability validation plan with 5–6 support ops participants to stress-test the end-to-end workflow.

STEP 01 · SIMULATION

Task-based incident simulation

Guide support ops leads through a full scenario—from spotting an SLA dip to isolating the cause, assigning an owner, and verifying resolution.

STEP 02 · SIGNAL CHECK

Signal-to-noise check

Verify whether the exception-first view speeds up triage or risks alert fatigue during high-volume operational spikes.

STEP 03 · STACK FIT

Integration & permissions pass

Confirm which data fields leads actually need from helpdesk and messaging tools so OpsPilot stays lightweight and non-duplicative.

SUCCESS FRAMEWORK (NDA)

Actual performance figures are redacted under NDA. Success is defined around four operational KPIs designed to close the loop from detection to resolution.

DIAGNOSIS SPEED Time to Root Cause

Reduction in median time from anomaly detection to identified root cause.

ACCOUNTABILITY Ownership Coverage

WORKFLOW CLARITY Tool Switching
