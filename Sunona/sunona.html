Sunona - TL;DR

  • What It Is: An inclusive audio brand and app bringing music, stories, and familiar sounds to children, adults, and seniors through cultural familiarity and effortless listening.
  • Brand & UX Voice: Warm, unhurried, and respectful-avoiding tech jargon or rushed prompts while keeping navigation simple across age groups.
  • System & App Screens: Pairs brand identity, usage guidelines, accessibility rules, and governance with tailored app screens for discovery, playback, and family/generational modes.
Children Playful stories & guided audio
Adults On-the-go music & narratives
Seniors Clear labels & calm controls
Governed Brand rules + A11y standards

01 · BRAND & UX VOICE

Rooted in cultural familiarity and warmth. Brand identity, guidelines, and UX tonality work together so the product feels welcoming rather than intimidating.

01 · BRAND IDENTITY

Warm, human & recognizable

Visual identity built around inviting color pairings, clear typography, and expressive audio motifs that feel warm and approachable across screens.

02 · BRAND GUIDELINES

Consistent rules across touchpoints

Clear guidance on logo usage, surface contrast, spacing, and visual hierarchy so every screen and communication stays cohesive as the system grows.

03 · UX TONALITY & VOICE

Calm, clear & culturally resonant

Conversational microcopy that respects the listener-using plain language, gentle guidance, and familiar cues without dense menus or technical clutter.

02 · EVERY GENERATION

Personalised content and simplified navigation. Adapts music, stories, and familiar sounds for children, adults, and seniors without fragmenting the core experience.

CHILDREN

Stories & playful discovery

Imaginative stories, gentle rhythms, and visual-first cues that make starting and switching audio easy for young listeners.

ADULTS

Music, narratives & routines

Curated listening for wind-down, daily routines, and familiar sounds-with quick access to saved picks and seamless playback.

SENIORS

Effortless & high-clarity controls

Generous tap targets, readable text, unmistakable play/pause states, and familiar cultural audio without complex settings.

03 · APP SCREENS & UX

Thoughtful interaction design for effortless listening. App screens keep browsing, mode selection, and playback calm and immediate.

SCREEN 01 · HOME

Generational & mood entry

Quickly jump into music, stories, or familiar sounds tailored to who is listening.

SCREEN 02 · DISCOVERY

Culturally familiar picks

Organized around relatable themes, moods, and everyday moments rather than endless feeds.

SCREEN 03 · PLAYER & PREFS

Uncluttered controls & comfort

Clear play/pause/skip controls and gentle adjustments for text size, voice tone, and categories.

04 · ACCESSIBILITY & GOVERNANCE

Inclusion is a foundation, not an afterthought. Accessibility standards and clear governance keep the brand and app reliable over time.

ACCESSIBILITY

Designed for comfort across abilities & ages

High-contrast surfaces, clear typographic hierarchy, large interactive targets, and non-color-only status indicators support comfortable use across generations.

GOVERNANCE

Shared standards for long-term consistency

Defines clear ownership and review rules for brand assets, tone of voice, and accessibility checks whenever new content or screens are added.
