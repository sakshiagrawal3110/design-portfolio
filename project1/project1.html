02 · DEFINE

"How might we let people decide, at the moment they pay, what their spending is working towards?" Anchored around Pay → Earn → Split → Redeem.

RULE 01 · MENTAL MATH

Simple math over tiers

Clear earn rates (UPI: 1 pt/₹10 · IMPS: 1 pt/₹25 · NEFT: 1 pt/₹50) and transparent floors let users calculate value in their head without fine print.

Movies: 250 Shopping: 500 Hotels: 2,000 Flights: 4,000
RULE 02 · TRUST FIRST

Money stays the hero

Trust in a financial product cannot be compromised for gamification. Every flow prioritizes payment confirmation before introducing rewards.

RULE 03 · CALM UX

Deferral over dark patterns

Users can skip splitting via a clean "Decide later" action—parking points in a calm Home banner with zero countdown timers or nagging.

03 · IDEATE & PATTERNS

Replaced separate transfer tabs and manual point-entry forms with two repeatable, low-friction interaction patterns.

PATTERN A · PAYMENT RAILS

Three rails, one pattern

Instead of three separate flows, a single segmented control dynamically swaps recipient fields, transfer limits, and live earn rates—so users learn one flow, not three.

04 · PROTOTYPE & CRAFT

Designed at a 390×844 mobile base around payment trust, goal-gradient momentum, and inclusive accessibility.

01 · HIERARCHY

Confirm money before rewards

The success screen leads with the green checkmark and transfer amount; only then does the "You earned X points" reward appear.

02 · GOAL-GRADIENT

Always-visible progress to unlock

Each wallet pairs a progress bar with plain-language copy ("150 to unlock") to pull users toward completing a goal.

03 · ACCESSIBILITY

Color never works alone

Every wallet pairs color with a distinct icon and label. Body text meets 4.5:1 contrast, and saffron is restricted to fills with dark ink—never as text on white.

05 · VALIDATION & ARCHITECTURE

Paired a targeted validation roadmap with production-grade fintech architecture built to scale for millions of users.

TEST & ITERATE · VALIDATION PLAN

Stress-testing pre-launch hypotheses

  1. Moderated usability testing (5 sessions): Measure friction and comprehension on the post-payment split screen.
  2. Threshold & copy testing: Validate whether "150 more to unlock" is immediately understood and motivates wallet focus.
  3. Edge-case & accessibility pass: Design onboarding/KYC, failed/pending states, points expiry, regional-language layouts, screen-reader order, dynamic type, and one-handed keypad reach.
SHIP & SCALE · SYSTEMS THINKING

Architected for real Indian payment rails

  • Payments & compliance: Third-party app provider via sponsor bank over NPCI (UPI) and bank-partner rails (IMPS/NEFT)—Spendit never holds funds.
  • Idempotent points ledger: Append-only, double-entry ledger ensures retried callbacks never double-award points.
  • Event & config-driven: Independent earning, splitting, and partner redemption events; adding a 5th category like Dining requires data + an icon, not a UI redesign.
  • Resilient on real networks: Explicit pending state re-sync, low-bandwidth core screens, and type support for Indian scripts.
